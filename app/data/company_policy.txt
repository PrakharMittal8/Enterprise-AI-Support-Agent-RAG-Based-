RETURN POLICY

Customers can return products within 10 days of delivery.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days after successful pickup verification.

If pickup status is disputed, escalation to logistics team is required.

Customers reporting fraud concerns should receive high-priority handling.

Repeated delivery failures qualify for compensation coupons.

Premium customers receive priority support response within 2 hours.

Damaged product complaints require image verification before refund approval.

Refunds above 500 INR require manager approval.

Orders marked as "Delivered" but disputed by customer require investigation.

Technical product issues are routed to technical support team.
